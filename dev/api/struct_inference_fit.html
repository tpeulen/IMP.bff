◆ stop_reason

std::string InferenceFit::stop_reason

why the fit stopped: "tolerance" (the decrement fell below the requested tolerance), "no_accepted_step" (the stepper found no improving step) or "max_iterations".

converged is also true for "no_accepted_step" and "max_iterations" when the last accepted decrement was below 1e-3 – the rule bayesian_decay_fit_node used and the stored results were made with. That is a weaker statement than the requested tolerance: use converged_at_tolerance and this field to tell them apart (PRD-156, drieger's review question under ucfret F1, 2026-11-11).
