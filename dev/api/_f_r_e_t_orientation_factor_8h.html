Turn a distribution into the distance-ratio distribution.

A FRET measurement does not see ; it sees an apparent distance. The two are related by – a low reads as a longer distance – and this performs that change of variable, Jacobian included.

The Jacobian is the point.** Transforming the abscissa and carrying the weights across unchanged would give a distribution that is wrong wherever the mapping is not linear, which is everywhere: . Weights are re-normalised after it is applied.

The transformed points are then resampled onto a uniform axis by linear interpolation, which is what makes the result convolvable with a distance distribution.

Parameters
[in]k2_ampamplitudes, non-negative, not required to be normalised
[in]k2_val values, strictly positive
[in]n_binspoints on the output axis
[out]out_view,n_out_viewthe axis (n_bins), then the interpolated weights (n_bins), then as a single trailing value
Exceptions
IMP::ValueExceptionon mismatched lengths, a non-positive , a negative amplitude, or zero total amplitude

One flat buffer rather than three returns, because a numpy view is one array. IMP.bff.photophysics.kappa2_to_distance_ratio is the front door and splits it into the (axis, weights, mean) tuple callers expect.

for diffusion with traps, given a measured efficiency.

Each dye is either freely diffusing – rotating fast enough to average its orientation, contributing – or trapped, wobbling in a cone set by its order parameter. sD2 and sA2 are the trapped fractions, so the pair splits into four sub-populations: free/free, trapped/trapped, trapped/free and free/trapped. Their efficiencies are combined and the result inverted into the single that would have produced it.

That inversion is what separates this from wobbling_kappa2_distribution(): it does not ask what the geometry gives, but what the geometry and the measured efficiency together imply.

Per sample: two isotropic directions are drawn, is taken along x, and the four sub-populations (donor free or trapped, acceptor free or trapped) are combined into one efficiency before being inverted back into a single .

Drawing the directions with three standard normals is load-bearing: the normalised components of a uniform draw fill the cube's positive octant, not the sphere, and that halved to 0.333 in the isotropic limit where it must be exactly 2/3.

[in]sD2,sA2second-rank order parameters of donor and acceptor
[in]fret_efficiencythe measured efficiency
[in]n_samplesorientation pairs to draw
[in]n_binsbins between k2_min and k2_max
[in]k2_min,k2_maxhistogram range
