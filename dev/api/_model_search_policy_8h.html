Train one move-scoring network over every episode, whatever its family.

Listwise: within an episode the moves' scores log(prior) + network(row) are softmaxed and the cross-entropy against the correct move is minimised with Adam over bff's MlpCore, plus weight_decay times the squared weights. Hidden layers are tanh, the output is linear and starts at zero, inputs are standardised. seed draws the weights and the split. A validation_fraction of episodes is never trained on: half of it picks the epoch to keep (early stopping), the other half is what the reported accuracies are measured on.
