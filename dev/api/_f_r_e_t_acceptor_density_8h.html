IMPBFF_BEGIN_NAMESPACE IMPBFFEXPORT double acceptor_characteristic_density ( double forster_radius,
int dimension )

C0, the density with one acceptor within R0 on average, in length^-d.

3-D: 1/(4/3 pi R0^3); 2-D: 1/(pi R0^2); 1-D: 1/(2 R0).

Exceptions
std::domain_errorunless R0 > 0 and d is 1, 2 or 3.

The quenched donor decay on t_i = i dt, from its donor-only lifetime spectrum.

sum_k a_k exp(-t/tau_k) times the quenching factor. With period > 0 the decays of the folded_periods preceding pulses are added, each quenched at its own elapsed time – folding the decay, which keeps the stretched term exact where folding a spectrum could not.
