Detailed Description

A natural cubic spline on uniformly spaced knots.

The free parameters are the knot heights mu_k = u(x_k); the spline is linear in them, u(x) = sum_k mu_k phi_k(x), and the basis functions phi_k depend on the knot geometry only. Outside the knot range the spline continues linearly (a natural spline has zero curvature at its ends).
