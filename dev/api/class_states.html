Detailed Description

A weighted set of states of one label.

The states are (x, y, z, weight) per point, flat. Everything a representation has to supply is here and nothing else is: the grid an accessible volume was enumerated on belongs to #IMP::bff::ProbeAccessibleVolume, and the conformers a rotamer library carries belong to the rotamer ensemble. Neither is part of what a distance or a rate needs.

The three distances live here rather than one level down for the same reason: they are functions of the cloud, so a rotamer ensemble and an accessible volume answer them identically.

◆ dRmp()

Distance between the two mean positions, A.

The cheapest of the three and the least meaningful: it is the distance between two averages, which is not the average of the distance and can sit several Angstrom from either of the others.
