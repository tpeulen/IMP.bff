A root of the scalar f in [a, b] by Brent's method.

SciPy's brentq (Charles Harris' C, scipy/optimize/Zeros/brentq.c), step for step, with its defaults. f is a MinimizeObjective evaluated at the one-element vector [x]. Throws ValueException when f(a) and f(b) have the same sign, as SciPy raises ValueError.
