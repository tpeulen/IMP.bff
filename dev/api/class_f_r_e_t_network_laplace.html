Detailed Description

Laplace approximation of a FRETNetworkModel posterior at a MAP.

Precision H = sum_segments s s^T + (-d^2 log p) over the free parameters (transformed scale), Sigma = H^-1; natural-scale sigmas by the delta method. With a landscape process, the band sigma_u(q) of the grid-mean-centred landscape (paper Eq. 22); per measurement with a landscape map, the band of r(q).
