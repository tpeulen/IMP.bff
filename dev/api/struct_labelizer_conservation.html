Detailed Description

Everything ConSurf reports for one position, as the conservation tables read it.

score is the normalised Rate4Site score (low = conserved); lower and upper bound its 25-75 % interval; the grades are ConSurf's 1 (variable) .. 9 (conserved), 0 when unknown; variety holds the amino acids seen at the position, one letter each; insufficient is ConSurf's confidence cut-off. A record imported from a B-factor PDB carries the score only.
