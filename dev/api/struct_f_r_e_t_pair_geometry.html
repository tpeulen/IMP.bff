Detailed Description

The pair geometry of two labelled ensembles: every (i, j) at once.

R and kappa2 are n1 * n2, row-major; weight is the normalised outer product of the two weight vectors. kappa2_avg is the weight-averaged orientation factor.

The separation vectors used to come back too, as (n1, n2, 3). Nothing ever read them – they were the largest allocation in the call, built for no consumer – so each one is formed and discarded.
