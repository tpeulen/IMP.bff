The exact FRET efficiency of an exchanging ensemble.

Solves the master equation of the labelled population rather than assuming a limit: with the transition probability matrix over dt, the rate matrix is and the integrated populations solve . The efficiency is — the fraction of excitations that leave by transfer.

The transpose is load-bearing.** P[i][j] is the probability of , so populations evolve as a row vector and solves the transposed system. Solving instead is right only for a symmetric , and for a real transition matrix it gives the wrong fast-exchange limit.
