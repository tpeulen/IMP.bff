Detailed Description

The positions a residue's quenching centre takes over its rotamers.

One residue of a structure, its crystal centre (the centroid of its quenching atoms as deposited) and the centres of the Dunbrack rotamers that fit, with their library probabilities renormalised over the ones that fit. When no rotamer fits, or the residue has no rotatable side chain this library can place, the ensemble is the crystal centre with probability one and from_library is false.
