End-to-end distance distribution of an Ising two-state Gaussian chain.

The tractable Ising-worm-like-chain form for a partially structured chain: every residue is structured (S) or unstructured (U) under a nearest-neighbour Ising Hamiltonian – coupling is the cooperativity , field the bias towards S – and each residue contributes a Gaussian bond of mean-square extension or .

Because the bonds are Gaussian the characteristic function factorises per residue, so the Boltzmann-weighted end-to-end is an exact 2x2 transfer-matrix product,

, ,

and follows from the isotropic inverse transform , taken on a n_k -point trapezoidal grid. With it reduces to gaussian_chain().

The product is formed as written, without rescaling between residues. That is deliberate rather than careless: is used only as the ratio , so a per-residue rescaling would cancel only if it were the same at every k – and a per-k rescaling silently changes the answer. The cost is that a long chain with a large coupling can overflow to a non-finite ; those entries end up zero, as they do in the numpy original this reproduces.
