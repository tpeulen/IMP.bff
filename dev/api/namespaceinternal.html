The time moments of the decay, by two linear solves instead of a run.

is the generator of , with d carrying and rate carrying (every rate, the radiative one included), so time is in steps. is symmetrisable in every flux form, and positive definite once some rate is non-zero, so each solve is a Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate gradient in the symmetric variables.
