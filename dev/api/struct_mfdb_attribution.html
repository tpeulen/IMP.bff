Detailed Description

Who made the data an artifact holds, and on what terms.

Attribution is per artifact, not per document, and that is not a hypothesis: a family container can hold libraries under different licences at once — the shipped dye and spin-label rotamers are GPL-3.0-only while the converted Dunbrack side-chain table is "free for academic use" — and a document-level licence would be wrong the moment two of them share a file.
