The same, leaving out every point whose confidence is below min_confidence: e.g. AlphaFold's pLDDT (the B-factor column of its models) with 70. Disordered tails and signal peptides otherwise take the softest modes; on the AlphaFold models of seven open/closed proteins, leaving out pLDDT < 70 raised the top-20 precision for pairs changing by > 5 A from 0.49 to 0.74 (AUC 0.795 -> 0.820), while pLDDT-weighted springs did not help. Pairs with a left-out point are NaN in get_pair_distance_fluctuations / get_pair_change_probabilities.
