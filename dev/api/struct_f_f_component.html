Detailed Description

A coarse-grained dye/protein system: sites, bonds, and their terms.

(formerly IMP/bff/ProbeForceField.h, now a section of this file)

The topology and parameters of one simulation system — what #IMP::bff::cgprobe builds, writes to mmCIF, reads back, and hands to IMP to become particles and restraints.

Every term is a named field of a typed record: a bond carries site_a, site_b, length and type_id by name, so what a slot means is stated rather than conventional, and a consumer reads system.bonds without having to guard against the key being absent.
