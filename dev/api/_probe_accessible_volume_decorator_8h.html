Fraction of a volume's weight within radius of the selected atoms.

How much of the volume touches something. selection is a #IMP::bff::SelectionExpression in either dialect – "resi 50-60" or "resid 50 to 60" – evaluated against hierarchy.

Parameters
[in]avthe volume
[in]hierarchywhere the reference atoms are selected from
[in]selectionthe selection expression; empty selects every atom
[in]radiusthe contact radius, A. It has to clear the volume's own exclusion: an accessible volume already excludes the structure's van der Waals envelope inflated by the dye radius and half the linker width, so no point of it is within about 5 A of any atom centre and a smaller radius returns zero for everything. 8 A is a reasonable "touching" for a 3.5 A dye.
Returns
the weight fraction in [0, 1]

Decorate a fresh particle as an AV, resample it, and read the map.

The AV is decorated onto its own particle with the source passed separately. Setting it up on the source particle leaves the resampled map at the coordinate origin — header origin (0, 0, 0), obstacles nowhere near the search region, and most of the grid reported accessible.

Parameters
[in]modelholds the obstacle hierarchy and the attachment particle
[in]source_particlethe attachment atom, a real XYZR in that model
[in]linker_length,linker_width,r1,r2,r3,disc_stepthe AV parameters
[in]allowed_sphere_radiusobstacles inside this radius of the attachment are ignored; without it the search starts inside the attachment atom's own neighbourhood and returns an empty volume, reporting nothing
[in]contact_volume_thickness,contact_volume_trapped_fractionthe ACV split, when one is wanted; see #IMP::bff::PathMap::apply_contact_weighting for the rule
[in]search_stencilDijkstra neighbour stencil. 0 leaves the decorator's own default, which is 342 — FPS's link search, the 3-voxel cube hop from every node. 74 is the seed-only "fast" search (the LabelLib reference metric); 26 is faster still: ~1.9x over 74 for ~20 % less volume, because a {1,2,3} stencil's isopath surface is cubic rather than spherical. 30 is the historical variant.
[in]radius_scalethe factor on IMP's radii as the obstacle set (#IMP::bff::AV_DEFAULT_RADIUS_SCALE, fitted to FPS)
Returns
the volume, carrying its cloud, its grid and the source coordinate

The flat-bottom well a measurement asks for, in mean-position coordinates.

The measurement's value and its asymmetric errors, each converted through rmp_from_measurement(): what comes back is – the bounds of the interval inside which a model is not penalised, and the centre.

The three are converted separately rather than the errors being carried across unchanged, because the relation is not linear: an error bar that is symmetric in the measured quantity is not symmetric in .

Parameters
[in]a,bthe two volumes
[in]measurementthe experimental distance and its errors
[in]accuracy,n_samples,seedas rmp_from_measurement()
Returns
three values: low bound, target, high bound. A bound that no separation reproduces is clamped to the reachable end rather than throwing – an error bar running past what the volumes allow is a one-sided restraint, not an error.

IMPBFFEXPORT double rmp_from_measurement ( const ProbeAccessibleVolumeDecorator & a,

Deterministic distance between two accessible volumes by lattice quadrature (PRD-105).

Each cloud is coarsened to at most quad_k weighted representative points (ProbeAccessibleVolumeDecorator::get_quadrature_points); the pair quantity is the weighted double sum over the two point sets, each term corrected to second order by the blocks' second central moments. Same number every call, independent of call order. Distance types as in av_distance(). Measured on T4L @ 2.0 A, K = 100: max error vs the exact double sum < 0.005 A on the mean distance (MC with 50k samples: ~0.25 A range).

Parameters
quad_kmaximum number of representative points per cloud The mean-position separation that would reproduce a measurement.

The one call an experiment-to-restraint conversion needs: two volumes and what was measured between them, in, and the to restrain to, out. It is #IMP::bff::rmp_from_model_distance() over the two volumes' point clouds, with the measurement's own distance convention and Förster radius, so a caller never has to unpack either.

Parameters
[in]a,bthe two volumes; they are resampled if they are stale
[in]measurementthe experimental distance to reproduce
[in]accuracystop when the model distance is this close, A
[in]n_samples,seedthe fixed pair sample the root is found on
Returns
the that reproduces measurement.distance
Exceptions
ValueExceptionwhen no separation reproduces it
See also
IMP::bff::effective_distance() for the forward, parametric approximation of the same relation.
