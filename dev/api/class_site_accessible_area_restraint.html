Detailed Description

Shrake-Rupley accessible area of one site per residue (Restraint).

Each site is sampled at radius on n_sphere points of #IMP::bff::sphere_points; a sample is buried when it lies within of another site closer than occluder_cutoff to its own. The score is the total area, , A^2 – a burial term whose weight sets its sign and strength. No derivatives: the area is a count of samples.

The arithmetic is get_site_accessible_area's; that function takes the occluder gate as a matrix (a caller's C-alpha distances), this one measures the sites. residue_solvent_accessible_surface is the exact form, where every site occludes.
