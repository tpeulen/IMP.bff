Hit records of an A3M alignment's rows, for #IMP::bff::select_sequence_homologs.

The first row is the query. For each other row: its match states as SequenceSearchHit::aligned, the aligned range on the query, its identity (identical pairs over the alignment's columns, insertions and deletions included), and the E-value when the row's header carries MMseqs2's fields (name score identity evalue qstart qend qlen tstart tend tlen), else 0.
