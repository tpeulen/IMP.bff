Four-channel tabulated backbone hydrogen-bond energy.

A residue donates through its H (with its N) and accepts through its O (with its C). For each gated residue pair both directions are tried; a direction d -> a counts when the donor's H index is positive, the acceptor's H index is non-zero, and cutoff_h2. Each counted bond adds table[c, floor(r / bin_width)] (skipped past the table end) for the channels c = 0: C_a-H_d, 1: N_d-O_a, 2: H_d-O_a, 3: N_d-C_a. The table is row-major . A direction whose residues lack the N, C or O atom (index < 0) is not a bond.

Returns
[n_bonds, energy]

Shrake-Rupley accessible area of sites, with occluders chosen by a gate.

Each present site is sampled at radius on the unit-sphere points (flat, three per point); a sample is buried when it lies within of the atom of another site with gate[i, j] < gate_cutoff (strict). Returns .

Not solvent_accessible_surface_area(): there every atom occludes, here only the gated sites do, which is the residue-level burial term a coarse-grained model scores.
