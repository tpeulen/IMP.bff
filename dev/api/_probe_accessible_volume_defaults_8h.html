How far, along an axis, the linker's first step reaches from its anchor, A.

FPS seeds a sphere (seed_radius, LinkerInitialSphere * W by default) and hops LinkSearchNodes voxels from every seed (av_routines.cpp:156-209): seed + 3 dg along an axis, further along a cube diagonal. PathMap::set_source_first_step takes the step itself; this is its straight-line extent, which a stencil does not walk.
