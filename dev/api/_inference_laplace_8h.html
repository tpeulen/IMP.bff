The exact curvature of minus the log posterior, by central differences of the graph's gradient.

H_ij = -(g_i(theta + h e_j) - g_i(theta - h e_j)) / 2h, h = rel * max(1, |theta_j|), symmetrised; 2 n_free gradient evaluations. The factors' information is Fisher's (expected) where a likelihood gives it; this is the observed curvature, which defines the Laplace approximation – they differ where a mode is not a perfect fit (PRD-143 A4.9: 11.7 nats of evidence on CBM56).

Polish a fit with the exact curvature: damped Newton steps on inference_hessian.

A scoring fit stops where its Fisher decrement is small, which is not always a point where the exact curvature is negative definite – and the exact-curvature evidence is only defined there. Stops at decrement tolerance or after max_iterations steps; laplace_evidence of the result is the exact-curvature one, iterations adds the polish steps to the fit's.

The posterior mean and sd of a positive functional f(theta) by Tierney & Kadane (1986).

functional is a score over scope (variable keys) whose eval returns [f, df/dx, -d2f/dx2] – the layout of a factor score, with f in place of the log density and its exact second derivative (negated) in place of the information. Two tilted fits, power * log(f + eps) added as a factor for power 1 and 2, start at mode.theta (the graph's mode, from inference_fitting, with its fixed variables held); both tilted integrals get the mode's own curvature plus the tilt's exact curvature at the tilted mode (bayesian_tierney_kadane, BayesianLaplace.h). The base curvature is the mode's information, or with exact_curvature inference_hessian.
