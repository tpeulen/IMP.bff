Give particles the decorators an integrator needs.

md wants a velocity, bd wants a diffusion coefficient – and the coefficient is Einstein's for the particle's own radius at this temperature, so a big atom diffuses more slowly than a small one without anybody saying so.

Parameters
[in]model,particleswhat to decorate
[in]temperatureK
[in]integratormd or bd
[in]radiione per particle; empty leaves an existing radius alone and gives an undecorated particle 1.7 A
