Detailed Description

Score the separation of two mean dye positions against a measurement.

Parameters
[in]mthe model the two particles belong to
[in]p1,p2the two dye particles; their XYZ coordinates are the mean positions
[in]measurementthe experimental distance, its asymmetric error, its Förster radius and which distance convention it is in
[in]sigmathe per-component width of the separation vector, for the conversion
[in]weightmultiplies the score
[in]max_forceFPS's MaxForce: past the restraint becomes linear rather than growing as a parabola (#IMP::bff::chi2_score_capped). 0, the default, is no cap and is the plain asymmetric this restraint has always scored; FPS's docking default is 400.
Note
The two particles are ParticleIndexAdaptor, which is how IMP::core's own restraints take theirs: a Particle, an AV decorator or a bare index all convert.
