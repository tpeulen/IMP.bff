Detailed Description

under a Gaussian pair model.

A lookup table over centre-to-centre distance: at each one the pair distance is distributed as the sum of two mirrored normals of width sigma, and the three conventions – mean distance, FRET-averaged distance, mean efficiency – are integrated over it once and interpolated afterwards.

This is what a docking run needs when it has an and an experiment that measured something else.
