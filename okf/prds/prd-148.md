# PRD-148: a smooth response for sampling -- the clamps become a soft floor

**Author:** Claude Opus 5 for tpeulen. **Written:** 2026-09-16 00:05 CEST.

**Owner** (tpeulen, ucfret prompt 472): "go" -- the first item of the list "what is missing in
bayesian decay?": smooth the response clamp, then dense metric adaptation (PRD-147 step 7), then
CBM56 chains from both modes, then lambda free.

## Why

`bayesian_response_basis` (`include/BayesianMeasuredResponse.h`) prepares a measured response in
four steps. Two of them are hard clamps at zero:
- the phase-ramp-shifted response, `y = max(y, 0)`, which removes the ringing of the Fourier shift
  (204 nats on the CBM56 global fit when absent);
- every convolved kernel column, `max(col, 0)`, before its unit sum.

A clamp is continuous, but its derivative jumps wherever a channel crosses zero, and the set of
channels that do so moves with the shift and the background fraction. The log posterior therefore
has kinks in exactly those coordinates. Two consequences:
- **Hamiltonian Monte Carlo sees them.** On the synthetic experiment
  (`test/decay/test_bayesian_decay_nuts.py`, strict xfail) NUTS reports 5-25 % divergent transitions
  in the response-shift and reference coordinates, with R-hat 1.06 and ESS 88.
- **The gradient is ill-defined at shift 0.** The header itself records that the FFT round trip
  returns +-1e-20 outside the support, so which zeros the clamp keeps is round-off.

A converged posterior on CBM56 needs a smooth target, so this comes before PRD-147 step 7.

**What is not known yet:** whether the clamps, and not something else, cause the divergences. Step 1
decides that, with a check that can come out either way.

## The design

A per-experiment choice of floor, `response_floor` in the manifest (`BayesianDecayExperiment`), passed
to `BayesianResponseOptions::floor`:
- **`hard`** (default): today's clamps, unchanged, so every existing gate (the 1e-12 torch parity,
  the CBM56 gates) keeps its meaning.
- **`soft`:** `s * sc * log1p(exp(y / (s * sc)))`, computed stably, with `sc = 1e-4 * max` of the
  quantity floored and `s = soft`. It uses the same scale and the same `soft` parameter as the
  background removal's floor, with no torch threshold, so it is C-infinity. The exact tangent is the
  logistic function of the same argument.
  - Its departure from the clamp is at most `s * sc * ln 2` per channel: 3.5e-6 of the peak at
    `s = 0.05`.
- **`none`:** no floor. Diagnosis only (step 1); not for results.

The kernel columns use the same floor as the response. The unit sums and the tangent bookkeeping are
unchanged.

## Steps, each with its check

1. **Diagnosis: is it the clamp?**
   - Build `floor` with `hard` and `none`, plus the manifest key.
   - On the synthetic experiment of `test_bayesian_decay_nuts.py`, run 4 NUTS chains x 300 warm-up
     x 400 draws (Laplace metric, same seeds and starts) under `hard` and `none`. Record divergent
     fractions after warm-up, the coordinates of the divergent transitions' starting points, worst
     rank R-hat and minimum bulk ESS.
   - *Declared before the run:* the clamp is the cause if `none` has < 0.5 % divergences and R-hat
     < 1.01 while `hard` has > 2 % divergences. If `none` also diverges, stop, record that, and look
     elsewhere (the reference coordinates, the soft floor of the background, the priors) before any
     step below.
   - *Done when* the table is in ucfret `okf/log.md` with a figure.
2. **The soft floor** (only if step 1 says the clamp).
   - *Done when:*
     - `soft` tangents agree with central differences of the basis to 1e-8 of each column's peak,
       at shift 0 and off it (the round-off caveat disappears);
     - `hard` output is bit-identical to today's (CBM56 gates at 1e-12 still pass);
     - `max |B_soft - B_hard|` is at most the bound above, per column.
3. **The synthetic posterior converges.**
   - `test_bayesian_decay_nuts.py` with `response_floor: soft`: rank R-hat < 1.01, bulk ESS > 100,
     zero divergences after warm-up. The strict xfail becomes a pass, and the `hard` run stays as
     the expected-fail control in the same test.
4. **CBM56 at the mode: soft does not change the answer.**
   - Fit D0+DA (the configuration that passes Rule 0) under `hard` and `soft`.
   - *Done when (declared):*
     - per histogram, |Delta D/dof| < 0.002;
     - the runs-test verdicts are unchanged;
     - the Laplace mean of p(R/R0) moves by less than 0.1 of its sd;
     - the figure shows both fits with weighted residuals.
   - If not, soft is a model change to be decided by tpeulen, not a smoothing.

Then PRD-147 step 7 (dense metric adaptation) runs on `soft`.

## Not in this PRD

Replacing the Fourier shift by an interpolating shift; the same question for the background floor's
torch threshold (a jump of 2e-9 * s, far below round-off in the likelihood); the red detectors.

## Outcome (2026-09-16 01:25, Claude Opus 5)

**Step 1 answered no: the clamp is not the cause.** Steps 2-4 were not done, as this PRD required.
The `soft` floor was started and removed again: its scale depended on the parameters, so its tangents
were wrong.

**What stays.**
- `response_floor` `hard` (default) and `none` (diagnosis only), in `BayesianResponseOptions::floor`
  and the manifest.
- The existing response and decay tests pass unchanged on `hard`.

**Measured** on the synthetic posterior (ucfret `s89_cpp/floor_diagnosis/`, figure
`fig/20260915T230257_s89_floor_diagnosis.png`). All runs: 4 chains x 300 warm-up x 400 draws, same
seeds and starts, Laplace metric.

| variant | divergent | worst rank R-hat | min bulk ESS |
|---|---|---|---|
| hard | 8.8 % | 1.27 | 13 |
| none | 21 % | 1.10 | 32 |
| hard, target acceptance 0.95 | 3.3 % | 1.19 | 15 |
| `log10_tau_ref` held at the mode | 32 % | 1.45 | 8 |
| reference spectrum sd widened to the grid spacing (0.356) | 6.2 % | 1.03 | 148 |

- **No kinks** along any coordinate: second differences at 1e-4 and 1e-2 sd agree.
- **The geometry is not Gaussian.** In whitened coordinates (Cholesky of the Laplace covariance, where a
  Gaussian has d2 = -1):
  - `log10_tau_ref`'s axis swings over -1.6e4..+2.1e4 within 3 sd;
  - `x_d0`'s over -352..+17 and `spec_eps`'s over -156..-2;
  - `irf_bg_gp`'s is +4.9 at the mode itself: the Fisher metric is not the Hessian there.
- **Interpretation**, not a proof: the synthetic divergences come from strongly varying curvature
  (reference-dye lifetime, a donor fraction near its bound, the spectrum weights), not from a
  non-smooth function. A different metric per region, or a reparameterisation of those coordinates,
  is what would address it.
- **The strict xfail stays.**

The CBM56 D0+DA posterior, the actual target, showed no divergences after warm-up in its first chain.
Its limit was the step size, so PRD-147 step 7 (dense metric adaptation) goes next. The whitened scan
is to be repeated on CBM56 first.
