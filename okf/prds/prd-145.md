# PRD-145: the sampled posterior of the Bayesian decay model, with convergence analysis

**Owner** (tpeulen, 2026-09-15, ucfret prompt 461): "go with sampling first, impl the convergence
analysis." **Author:** Claude Opus 5 for tpeulen.

## Why

No interval, posterior mean or band counts without sampled, converged chains (ucfret Rule 0c):
multiple chains from dispersed starts, split R-hat against a threshold declared before the run,
and ESS with the autocorrelation time beside every interval, computed on identified quantities.
On CBM56 the Laplace approximation has visibly failed:
- the delta-method bands of p(R/R0) go below zero;
- the exact-Hessian evidence is indefinite at a lambda node;
- PSIS importance weights collapsed (ESS 1/400).

The P-spline weight lambda has to be sampled jointly, because its joint mode is degenerate
(PRD-143 A4.6).

## What exists, checked 2026-09-15

- **Samplers:** bff's `MCMCSampler` (compiled) with "stretch", "de", "slice" and a blocked
  "metropolis" that takes a plain log-posterior function (`set_objective_function`) and a full
  proposal covariance (`set_proposal_covariance`); linking a driver against `libimp_bff` works.
  - No gradient-based sampler exists in bff or tttrlib.
  - **Decision here:** use the existing blocked Metropolis with the Laplace covariance as proposal
    (Roberts & Rosenthal 2001 scaling, which the sampler implements), not a new sampler.
  - If its autocorrelation time makes the ESS targets unreachable within a declared budget, HMC is
    proposed as a separate PRD, not added here.
- **Diagnostics:** no R-hat, ESS or autocorrelation estimator in bff or tttrlib (searched).
  arviz 1.3 is installed and is the vetted reference.

## The diagnostics

Rank-normalised split R-hat and ESS (Vehtari, Gelman, Simpson, Carpenter & Buerkner, Bayesian
Analysis 16, 667 (2021)), as arviz and Stan compute them:
- **R-hat:**
  - split chains;
  - rank-normalise the pooled draws (fractional ranks through the normal quantile function);
  - bulk R-hat on the normalised draws, tail R-hat on the normalised folded draws |x - median|;
  - reported R-hat = max(bulk, tail).
- **ESS:**
  - bulk ESS on the rank-normalised split chains;
  - tail ESS = min of the ESS of the 5 % and 95 % quantile indicators;
  - autocorrelation combined across chains (Stan's formula) and truncated by Geyer's initial
    positive and monotone sequence.
- **MCSE** of the mean; the integrated autocorrelation time tau = n_draws / ESS per chain.

The normal quantile function is Wichura's AS241 (Appl. Statist. 37, 477 (1988)); the autocovariance
is computed directly, lag by lag, until the Geyer sequence stops.

## Steps, each with its check

1. **tttrlib `modules/math/include/McmcDiagnostics.h`** (header-only, neutral names):
   `split_rhat`, `rank_normalized_split_rhat` (bulk, tail), `ess_bulk`, `ess_tail`, `ess_mean`,
   `mcse_mean`, `normal_quantile`; vendored into bff and pinned.
   - *Done when:*
     - AS241 matches its published table values;
     - on AR(1) chains with known rho the ESS is within 10 % of n (1 - rho)/(1 + rho);
     - on chains with shifted means the R-hat is > 1.1 (the control);
     - bff test `test/decay/test_mcmc_diagnostics.py` agrees with arviz (`rhat`, `ess` bulk and
       tail, `mcse`) to 1e-6 relative on random, AR(1) and non-mixing chain sets.
2. **bff `BayesianDecaySampling.h`: `bayesian_decay_sample`**. Runs `n_chains` Metropolis chains on
   the decay log posterior:
   - the proposal covariance is the Laplace covariance at the best mode;
   - starts are dispersed: mode + 2 L xi with seeded xi, plus, where given, the declared starts'
     end points, so a second mode is not hidden;
   - warm-up is discarded, draws are thinned, and every chain is kept.
   Its summaries are computed per draw on identified quantities: mean and sd of R/R0,
   p(R/R0) quantiles at the grid, x_d0, g, log10 lambda. Each summary carries R-hat, ESS
   bulk/tail, tau and MCSE.
   - *Done when* a synthetic experiment's sampled mean matches the analytic Laplace mean within 3 MCSE
     where the posterior is near Gaussian, and a deliberately non-mixed run (one chain at a second
     mode, no warm-up) is flagged.
3. **CBM56** (ucfret `s89_cpp`), D0 + DA (Rh110 excluded, prompt 459), lambda free on [-2, 5]:
   - thresholds declared before the run: R-hat < 1.01, bulk and tail ESS > 400 on every summary;
   - 4 chains;
   - the budget declared in the driver.
   - *Done when* the figure (traces, rank plots per chain, sampled p(R/R0) bands against the Laplace
     ones, the posterior of log10 lambda, a table of R-hat, ESS and tau) and the log entry exist,
     and the run either passes, or is excluded and counted with the failing summaries named.
