# PRD-146: the No-U-Turn sampler for the Bayesian decay posterior

**Owner** (tpeulen, 2026-09-15, ucfret prompt 463): "try NUTS." It follows PRD-145, where both samplers
bff already has fail on CBM56:
- the blocked random-walk Metropolis: tau ~13 000 log posteriors per independent draw;
- the ensemble slice sampler: more than 400 000.

**Author:** Claude Opus 5 for tpeulen.

## The algorithm, and where it lives

NUTS as Stan runs it:
- **Trajectory:** Hamiltonian dynamics by leapfrog under a fixed Euclidean metric, doubled forwards
  or backwards at random until the trajectory makes a U-turn (Hoffman & Gelman, JMLR 15, 1593 (2014)).
- **Termination criterion:** Betancourt's generalised one, on the summed momentum `rho`
  ("A Conceptual Introduction to Hamiltonian Monte Carlo", arXiv:1701.02434, appendix A.4.2),
  checked across each subtree merge and its sub-subtrees as Stan does.
- **Sampling:** multinomial sampling of the new state within a subtree, and biased progressive
  sampling between subtrees.
- **Divergence:** declared when the energy error exceeds 1000.
- **Step size:** adapted during warm-up by Nesterov dual averaging to a target acceptance statistic
  of 0.8 (Hoffman & Gelman, Algorithm 5), then frozen.
- **Metric:** dense, the inverse of a given covariance. Here that is the Laplace covariance at the
  best mode, which is the preconditioner the fit already computes.

It is generic (a log density and its gradient in, draws out) and header-only, so it goes to
**tttrlib** (`modules/math/include/NoUTurnSampler.h`, neutral names), vendored into bff and pinned --
the placement rule for simple, header-only algorithms (tpeulen, prompt 448).

## Steps, each with its check

1. **tttrlib `NoUTurnSampler.h`** with tttrlib test `test/cpp/test_no_u_turn_sampler.cpp`.
   - *Done when:*
     - on a 20-d correlated Gaussian (condition 1e4, identity metric) the sample means and variances
       are within 4 MCSE of the truth, with R-hat < 1.01 over 4 chains (`McmcDiagnostics.h`);
     - with the dense metric set to the true covariance, the tree depth drops and the ESS per
       gradient rises (the metric is used);
     - on a funnel-like target with too large a fixed step size, divergences are reported (the check
       can fail);
     - a sampler with the U-turn criterion disabled at a fixed depth of 1 (a plain random walk of one
       leapfrog) gives a far smaller ESS (the control).
   - Vendored into bff and pinned.
2. **bff: the decay posterior's gradient without the information matrix**
   (`bayesian_decay_log_posterior_and_gradient`, in `BayesianDecayPosterior.h`), and
   `bayesian_decay_nuts` running chains on it.
   - *Done when* the gradient equals `bayesian_decay_evaluate`'s to 1e-12 and costs less, and on the
     synthetic experiment, sampling from a start near the mode gives means within 4 MCSE of a long
     reference run with R-hat < 1.01.
3. **CBM56** (ucfret `s89_cpp`), D0 + DA (Rh110 excluded), lambda held first.
   - 4 chains: 2 dispersed around the best mode, 2 started from the second mode's end point.
   - Thresholds declared before the run: R-hat < 1.01, bulk and tail ESS > 400 on mean R/R0, sd R/R0,
     x_d0, g and p(R/R0) at its quartile grid points.
   - Reported: divergences, tree depths, and the time per chain.
   - *Done when* the figure (traces per chain, rank histograms, sampled p(R/R0) against the Laplace
     band) and the log entry exist, and the run either passes, or is excluded and counted with its
     failing summaries named.
   - If lambda-held sampling passes, lambda is freed in a second run.

## Progress

- **Step 1 done, 2026-09-15.** tttrlib 0462f6704 `NoUTurnSampler.h`, vendored and pinned.
  - Tests on a 20-d Gaussian with condition 1e4, 4 chains:
    - means within 2.5 MCSE and variances within 2.9 MCSE of the truth;
    - R-hat 1.005, bulk ESS >= 1013;
    - with the true covariance as metric, the tree depth drops by 3.6 and the ESS per gradient rises 152x;
    - the max-depth-1 control keeps 0.4 % of the ESS;
    - Neal's funnel with a fixed step of 0.8 reports 208 divergences.
  - Found on the way and fixed in `McmcDiagnostics.h`: an unsigned `n - 3` in the Geyer loop read past
    the array with fewer than 4 draws per split chain. It is now pinned by a 5-draw arviz case.
- **Step 2 done, with one finding.**
  - `bayesian_decay_log_posterior_and_gradient` equals `bayesian_decay_evaluate` to 2e-16 of the peak
    (6.2 ms against 7.4 ms on CBM56).
  - `BayesianDecaySampling.h`: `bayesian_decay_nuts_chain` and `bayesian_convergence`.
  - On the synthetic experiment, two independent 4-chain runs agree on every mean (worst 2.1 combined
    MCSE), and a 5-draw run is flagged (R-hat 2.8).
  - They do not converge. At target 0.8, 5-25 % of draws diverge: R-hat 1.06, ESS 88. At target 0.95
    fewer diverge, but R-hat is 1.26 and ESS 12.
  - The divergent draws sit in the response-shift, reference-lifetime and reference-scatter coordinates,
    where this experiment clamps its shifted response at zero on purpose. The posterior has kinks there.
  - Test `test_bayesian_decay_nuts.py`: the convergence check is marked as a strict expected failure.
- **Step 3 first chain (CBM56, D0 + DA, lambda held; 150 warm-up, 150 draws, 843 s).**
  - Sampler: step size 0.011, mean tree depth 8.7, 451 gradients per draw, no divergences after warm-up
    (17 during).
  - Draws: log posterior about 70 below the mode, the typical set of 136 dimensions.
  - Mixing: tau of mean R/R0 1.6 draws, sd R/R0 1.4, g 2.6, x_d0 8.4.
  - Cost per independent draw of mean R/R0: about 4.5 s, against about 29 s for the random-walk
    Metropolis and more for the ensemble slice.
  - ESS 400 on x_d0 would take about 2.6 h for 4 chains at this step size.
  - The small step (a Laplace metric that fits the posterior poorly) is the cost. Stan's windowed
    metric adaptation from warm-up draws is the standard remedy and is not yet implemented.
