## Why

The analysis that fits CBM56 (donor-only, FRET and Rh110 samples, polarised,
both pulse windows, measured water responses per acquisition) lives in three
places: the ucfret Python prototype (`s88_laplace_posterior.py`, imported by
`examples/spectroscopy/bayesian_decays/cbm56.py` from the ucfret checkout),
its C++ port in ucfret (`s89_cpp/cbm56_model.h`, `cbm56_fit.h`), and -- for the
generic pieces only -- bff (`BayesianFisherScoring.h`, `BayesianLaplace.h`,
`Optimization.h`). The C++ belongs here, under the language rule, with its
tests here; ucfret keeps only the parity gates against its Python prototype.

## What moves, and what it is called

Flat, `Bayesian*` vocabulary (PRD-141). Header-only, `IMP::bff`, std + the
headers bff already carries (`internal/json.h`) + pocketfft through tttrlib
(`"pocketfft/pocketfft_hdronly.h"`, tttrlib's `thirdparty/` on the include
path, as tttrlib's own sources include it).

| step | header | what |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `BayesianMeasuredResponse.h` | a measured response prepared for convolution: its fitted background removed under a soft floor over its support, a fractional circular shift by a phase ramp clamped at zero, unit sum; the exact bin-integrated periodic kernel per grid lifetime (its FFT cached per axis and grid); the basis `[response | kernel columns | flat]`; forward-mode tangents in the background and the shift |
| 2 | `BayesianGoodnessOfFit.h` | Poisson deviance per degree of freedom, the runs test on residual signs (statsmodels `runstest_1samp`, cutoff mean, no correction), the deviance reference measured by drawing Poisson data at the fitted means |
| 3 | `BayesianDecayModel.h` | the experiment as typed tables (scopes, parts, responses, variables, priors -- read once from a manifest), theta -> constrained values, the physics and instrument amplitudes, their analytic Jacobian, expected counts and the count Jacobian |
| 4 | `BayesianDecayPosterior.h` | prior (per-variable families, P-spline, spectrum continuity) with gradient and Hessian, Poisson likelihood, gradient and Fisher matrix (through `BayesianFisherScoring.h` where it fits), node fit through `DampedNewton` (`line_search_below`), Laplace evidence (`BayesianLaplace.h`), p(R/R0) with its delta-method band |
| 5 | -- | ucfret's `s89_cpp` includes these headers and deletes its own; its gates against the Python prototype (1e-12 per stage, the fit within 0.05 nats / 1e-4 / 1e-3) and the 3 s must still pass |
| 6 | (optional, needs a build) | `imp_bff bayesian-decay fit <fixture>` subcommand |

A shared concern, recorded not decided: bff's graph now prepares a response a
second way (`internal/ResponseFunction.h`: constant background, clip, window,
linear-interpolation shift, tttrlib's recursion -- ChiSurf's semantics). Step 1
does not change it. Whether the graph's `TCSPCDecay`/`Convolution` gain these
numerics as modes, or this analysis moves onto the graph, is the step after
this PRD and a decision with that code's owner.

## Tests (all in bff, Python, compiling a C++ driver header-only)

The drivers compile with `c++ -std=c++17` against `include/` (through an
`IMP/bff` shim, as `expensive_test_standalone_core_compiles.py` does),
`standalone/include/` and tttrlib's `thirdparty/`; a test skips when no
compiler or no tttrlib checkout is found. Every test has a case that must
fail.

1. `test/decay/test_bayesian_measured_response.py`: the tangents against central
   differences at a non-zero shift (1e-7 of the peak); the same comparison
   with the clamp's mask dropped from the tangent must disagree (negative
   control); every kernel column sums to one; the exact periodic kernel
   against a 16x oversampled brute-force convolution re-binned (1e-4 of the
   peak) -- and the trapezoid kernel must NOT meet that (it did not, 1-1.6 %,
   which is why the exact one exists); a shift by one whole bin equals a
   circular roll.
2. `test/decay/test_bayesian_goodness_of_fit.py`: runs-test p against
   statsmodels on random and on structured sign sequences (1e-12), skipped
   without statsmodels; the deviance reference's mean near 1 for data drawn
   from the model; a model off by a factor 1.05 fails it.
3. `test/decay/test_bayesian_decay_model.py`: on a small synthetic experiment
   built in the test (random transfer tensors, 3 scopes, 2 detectors): the
   amplitude and count Jacobians against central differences (1e-7), a scope
   table keyed wrongly must change the amplitudes.
4. `test/decay/test_bayesian_decay_posterior.py`: prior gradient and Hessian
   against differences; data simulated from the synthetic model at a known
   theta, fitted from a perturbed start: converged, D/dof within the measured
   reference (|z| < 3), the evidence finite; with the data from a DIFFERENT
   theta (a lifetime off by 20 %) the deviance test must fail.
5. `test/decay/expensive_test_bayesian_decay_cbm56.py`: the CBM56 fixture from
   the environment (`BFF_CBM56_FIXTURE`, written by ucfret's
   `emit_cbm56_fixture.py`), skipped without it: the fit against the fixture's
   polished Python mode at step 5's tolerances.
